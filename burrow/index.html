    How to play

    • Seven letters a day, the same for everyone, and one minute once you start.
    • Make words of 4 to 7 letters, using each letter once in a word: tap the letters (or type them), then Enter. Tap a letter again to put it back.
    • Every word digs Clover deeper, and the rarer the word, the deeper: note 4 m, tenon 15 m. How rare comes from how often a word turns up in English. Longer words dig a little more, and a word using all seven letters 10 m more.
    • Stuck? Shuffle the letters.
    • How deep can you get? Topsoil, Root Cellar, Clay Seam, Bedrock, or all the way to the Glowworm Cave.
    • Want more? Practice rounds use other letters, as many as you like, and don't count toward your stats.
