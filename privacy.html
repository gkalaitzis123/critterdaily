Privacy

No accounts, and cookies only to count visits

Critter Daily's games don't ask who you are. Your progress stays on your device; what leaves it are the anonymous counts, scores and Deep Cut answers below. The only cookies are Google Analytics', to count visits, and in the EU, the UK and Switzerland only if you say yes.

What stays on your device

Each game keeps your progress, stats and settings (like metric or US units) in your own browser's storage, so you can come back to today's game and keep your streak. Clearing your browser's data for this site erases them.

Fonts

The pages load their fonts (Fredoka and Nunito) from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, as it does on many websites. See Google Fonts' privacy notes.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, sees the requests your browser makes. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Counting visits and games

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics and Umami, neither of which uses cookies or follows you from site to site. They note which pages are viewed, the site that linked here, the kind of browser and device, the country and how fast the pages load. None of it identifies you. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and Umami's privacy policy.

Google Analytics

We also count visits with Google Analytics, because advertisers judge a site by it, and ads may one day help keep the games free. It uses cookies to tell one visit from the next, and tells Google the pages you view, the site that linked here, your browser and device, and roughly where you are (Google doesn't give us your address). For the games, it notes when one is started, finished or shared, with its score; when you finish a day's game, on how many days you've played it and how many in a row; and on the home page, whether you've played before. Those are just numbers, counted on your device, so we can see how the games go and how many players come back. Ad cookies are off. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland we ask first, and it sets no cookies unless you say yes; elsewhere it's on unless you turn it off. Turn it on or off any time with Count my visits in Settings (the sliders button on every page). See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How everyone did

When you finish a day's game, its score goes to Critter Daily's own small database on Cloudflare, so the results can show how everyone did that day. So that each connection counts once a day, the score is kept with a code scrambled from your connection's address, a secret and the date, which can't be turned back into the address or matched from one day to the next. Practice rounds aren't sent.

Deep Cut also sends, for each prompt of a day's game, the answer that counted (if any) and the guesses that weren't on the list, kept with the same scrambled code and nothing else about you. They show how many players named each answer, and the guesses several players try that aren't on a list tell us what to add. Practice rounds aren't sent either.
