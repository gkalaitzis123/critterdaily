Privacy

No accounts, no tracking

Critter Daily's games don't ask who you are, don't use cookies, and don't send your guesses or scores anywhere.

What stays on your device

Each game keeps your progress, stats and settings (like metric or US units) in your own browser's storage, so you can come back to today's game and keep your streak. Clearing your browser's data for this site erases them.

Fonts

The pages load their fonts (Fredoka and Nunito) from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, as it does on many websites. See Google Fonts' privacy notes.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, sees the requests your browser makes. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
