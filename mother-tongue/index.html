      How to play

      • Everyone gets the same mystery language each day. It's waiting in an egg at the top of the family tree.
      • Guess any language: type it, or tap the box to scroll through the 600 best known. Pip hops to the deepest branch it shares with the mystery: guess Spanish when it's Portuguese, and you learn they're both West Iberian.
      • Each guess also shows how far away the mystery is spoken, and in which direction, even when they aren't related.
      • The closer you get, the more the egg cracks. Type a branch's name, like Bantu, to see the languages in it.
      • After 5, 10 and 15 guesses you get a hint. You have 20 guesses in all: the fewer you need, the better your feather.
      • Want more? Practice with a random language as often as you like. Practice games don't count toward your stats.
