      How to play

      • Ten questions a day, the same for everyone, each with a number for an answer: how many people live somewhere, how many species of beetle there are, the year a battle was fought or a landmark opened, how many people a disaster killed, the longest a lion has lived.
      • Guess as close as you can. Spot on earns 10 acorns, and you earn fewer the further off you are.
      • How close is close depends on the question. A film's running time needs to be within a few minutes, and a population or a number of species within about half or double. A year needs to be within a few years if it's recent, and gets more room the longer ago it was, up to 80 years for ancient history.
      • After each guess, a number line shows the answer, where your guess landed, and the green band that earns 7 acorns or more.
      • Lengths and areas can be answered in meters or feet, kilometers or miles: pick under the box.
      • Your acorns add up to 100. Nut Nibbler, Acorn Gatherer, Stash Keeper, Hoard Master or Squirrel Sage?
      • Want more? Practice rounds use random questions, as many as you like, and don't count toward your stats.
