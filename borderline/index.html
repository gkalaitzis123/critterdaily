        About Borderline

        How to play Borderline, the daily history map game

        Each day there are three mystery places, the same for everyone and easiest first: countries, empires, kingdoms or other states, each at one moment in history. You see the year, and the land shaded on a map of that year, and you have 6 guesses for each. Guess any place from any time: each guess shows how far away the mystery is from it and in which direction, and whether your guess existed in that year, or how many years too early or too late it was. Homer the homing pigeon flies toward the mystery as you get closer.

        Are there hints?

        Yes. After 2 wrong guesses you learn which of today's countries the land is in, after 3 the map names the other states of that year, and after 4 you get the first letter of its name.

        Where do the borders come from?

        From Cliopatria, the Seshat Global History Databank's open atlas of the world's states from 3400 BCE to today (CC BY 4.0). Borders long ago are uncertain, and the atlas shows one version of them.

        How to play

        • Everyone gets the same three mysteries each day, easiest first: a country, empire or kingdom at one moment in history. You see the year, and its land shaded on a map of that year.
        • Guess any place from any time: type it, or tap the box to scroll through the best known. Each guess shows how far away the mystery is, and which way.
        • It also shows whether your guess existed in that year, or how many years too early or too late it was.
        • After 2, 3 and 4 wrong guesses you get a hint: which of today's countries the land is in, the names of the other states that year, and the first letter of its name.
        • You have 6 guesses for each. Find all three in as few as you can for the best title, from Wanderer to Master Cartographer.
        • Want more? Practice with three random places as often as you like. Practice games don't count toward your stats.
