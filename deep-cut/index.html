          How to play

          • Each day has five prompts, like Name a cheese. Everyone gets the same ones.
          • You have 30 seconds to type any answer that fits. Wrong guesses cost nothing but time.
          • Your first right answer is locked in. The rarer it is, the deeper Digby digs: Common 10 m, Uncommon 25 m, Rare 45 m, Very rare 70 m, and a Deep cut 100 m.
          • Rarity comes from how often each answer's Wikipedia page is read, ranked within the list. The first few steps down the list count the most, so you don't need the most obscure answer to score well.
          • Your five answers add up. Every 100 m is a new layer, from Topsoil Tourist down to Core Explorer.
          • Want more? Practice rounds use random prompts, as many as you like, and don't count toward your stats.
