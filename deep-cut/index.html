          How to play

          • Each day has five prompts, like Name a Roman emperor or Name a bone in the human body. Everyone gets the same ones.
          • You have 30 seconds to type any answer that fits. Spelling needn't be perfect: small typos count, and if you're close, Digby asks "Did you mean…?" Wrong guesses cost nothing but time. Your first right answer is locked in.
          • Then we look at its Wikipedia page. Of everyone who read the pages of all the prompt's answers over the last 60 days, what share read yours? Augustus is 1 in 11; Trebonianus Gallus is 1 in 240.
          • Every ten times rarer digs 10 m deeper: 1 in 10 is 10 m, 1 in 1,000 is 30 m, 1 in 100,000 is 50 m.
          • Your five answers add up, and Digby digs back through Earth's history: the Holocene, the Pleistocene, the Cretaceous, the Jurassic, the Permian and, deepest of all, the Cambrian.
          • Want more? Practice rounds use random prompts, as many as you like, and don't count toward your stats.
